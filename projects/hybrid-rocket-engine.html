500 N Hybrid Rocket Engine

Capstone design project: my team designed a 500 N thrust hybrid rocket engine, and I was responsible for its structural and mechanical design.

When: Bachelor's capstone Role: Structural and mechanical design Context: Course project, York University
PropulsionNX (FEA)SolidWorksMachining

Motivation

For our capstone project, my team designed a hybrid rocket engine with a target thrust of 500 N, taking it from concept through detailed design and manufacturing plans.

My role

  • Structural design of the engine.
  • CAD models and engineering drawings in SolidWorks.
  • Finite element analysis in NX 12.0.
  • Manufacturing and assembly plans, plus other mechanical aspects of the design.

Skills

  • Structural design
  • CAD and engineering drawings (SolidWorks)
  • Finite element analysis (NX 12.0)
  • Design for manufacturing and assembly
  • Machining on a mill and lathe

Outcome

COVID-19 lockdowns prevented the team from testing the engine experimentally. The project still deepened my CAD and FEA skills, and taught me to machine parts on a mill and lathe.
