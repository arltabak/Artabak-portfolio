About

I work in R&D in Ontario, with a background in space engineering. My experience spans mechanical design, structural and thermal FEA, mold flow analysis, and 3D-printed prototyping.

I hold an M.Sc. from York University, where I worked in the Responsive Materials & Robotics Laboratory, and I enjoy building tools that make engineering analysis faster and more reliable. Outside work, I cook, bake, and barbecue.
